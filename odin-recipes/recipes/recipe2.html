Chicken Adobo

Description

Chicken Adobo is a popular Filipino dish made with chicken, soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and spices.

It has a savory and slightly sour taste. It is commonly served with steamed rice.

Ingredients

  • 1 kilogram chicken
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup vinegar
  • 1 cup water
  • 6 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon peppercorns
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil

Steps

  1. Wash and prepare the chicken.
  2. Marinate the chicken with soy sauce and garlic for 30 minutes.
  3. Heat cooking oil in a pot.
  4. Brown the chicken on both sides.
  5. Add the marinade, vinegar, and water.
  6. Add bay leaves and peppercorns.
  7. Cover the pot and simmer until the chicken is tender.
  8. Serve hot with steamed rice.
